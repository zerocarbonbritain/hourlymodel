# 2018 baseline: rebuild notes

[baseline_2018.json](../scenarios/baseline_2018.json) is generated by `baseline/build.py` with the same input files as the 2024 baseline, for calendar year 2018. Every input and its source cell is listed in [baseline/reports/baseline_2018.md](../baseline/reports/baseline_2018.md). The method is described in [updating-the-baseline.md](updating-the-baseline.md) and [baseline-2024-notes.md](baseline-2024-notes.md).

The sources are the current editions (DUKES 2026, ECUK 2025, greenhouse gas statistics 1990 to 2024, NTS 2024, TSGB 2025, ONS mid 2025 release), read for 2018. They include revisions since the original workbook, so values differ even where the method is the same.

## Main changes from the original 2018 baseline

- **Population** 66,287,000 (revised mid 2018 estimate, was 66,460,000). **Households** 27,576,000.
- **Supply.** Nuclear 9.26 GW at 80.2% (DUKES 5.7, was 11.64 GW at 83.5%, which overstated generation). Solar 13.06 GW (DUKES 6.2, was 15.4). Solar availability is calibrated, so modelled solar is 11.1%, as observed. Net imports of 19.1 TWh are dispatched with backup, up to 4.04 GW of interconnector capacity (DUKES 5.13A).
- **Heating.** Shares and GW/K follow the 2024 method, with heat pump heat calibrated to DUKES 6.1 ambient heat. Services catering gas is 11.1 TWh (was 16.42).
- **Transport.** Miles per person keep the original 2018 values adjusted for revised passenger km and population. Car EV share 0.22% (battery electric stock share times 1.32, was 1.25%). Engine efficiencies are calibrated to DUKES and ECUK C8 fuel, which gives cars 0.1975 (was 0.2191) and freight 173.7 TWh (was 188.1).
- **Industry.** `refinery_electric_TWhy` is 7.0 TWh (was 12.6, which double counted generation own use). Other values follow the current ECUK U4.
- **Backup and fuels.** Fuel shares and efficiencies from DUKES 5.6 (coal plus other thermal 12.0% at 30.4%). `other_gas_use` 4.7, `other_oil_use` 17.8 and `other_coal_use` 1.2 TWh follow the 2024 definitions (were 14.8, 31.8 and 7.0 as workbook corrections). Energy industry use and gas losses are calibrated to DUKES 1.1. Storage is 3.4 GW (DUKES 5.16).
- **Emissions balance.** From the current inventory with the 2024 mapping. Ammonia 0.837 MtCO2e. Land use fields change substantially with the revised peatland reporting (grassland 0.31, was -9.17).

## Validation

```
Electricity demand (TWh/yr): 344.6
Onshore wind CF: 25.8%
Offshore wind CF: 37.0%
Solar PV CF: 11.1%
Space heating demand (TWh/yr): 366.6
Water heating demand (TWh/yr): 84.1
Industry demand (TWh/yr): 223.5
Total emissions (MtCO2e): 526.4
Grid CO2 intensity (gCO2/kWh): 200.6
```

Total emissions are 526.4 MtCO2e against 507.0 in the statistics including international aviation and shipping, plus the model's aviation non CO2 uplift of 29.1, so 536.1. The model is 1.8% low, as for 2024. Modelled backup generation is 185.5 TWh against 191.1 TWh observed. Grid CO2 intensity on the DUKES 5.14 basis (UK generation less own use) is 222 against 221.0 gCO2/kWh published.
